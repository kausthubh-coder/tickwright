Details behind each choice

Story the Wilt, the four withered gods, who you meet

A drought god swallowed the rain and the islands crusted over into the grey Wilt. You wake the last spring under an old shrine and its spirit, Mizu, fills your sprayer. Mizu speaks rarely; no Paimon-style narrator.

  • Tsuru, the shrine keeper, tried to seal the god once and failed. She teaches you farming and hides why she failed.
  • Kaito, a forge-village rival, thinks the drought stops the floods that drowned his home. He becomes an ally in chapter 2.
  • Each region holds one withered god. Beating it restores that season and its crops: spring rice, summer peppers, autumn pumpkins, winter frost melons.
  • Villagers return as land is restored. Their friendship scenes (under 60 s, skippable) teach combo moves, as Persona's Confidants do.
  • After every expedition, win or lose, the village plays a short new exchange, as Hades does after each run.
World and day one island, four-minute crossing, 20-minute day

One hand-made island about 1.2 km across: a central village and four season regions. Walking across takes about 4 minutes; sliding on wet ground is 1.8× faster. Hills hide the next landmark, a reward is visible from every peak, and each region has a purification meter.

Day and night are separate phases because mixing them failed for Dave the Diver: its director "couldn't focus on either task" until he split them.

Combat and farm numbers spray, dodge, seeds, stats
Spray
1 water per tick at 20 ticks/s, about 6 damage, 0.6 m splash. Tank 100. Hits paint the ground wet.
Slide
On wet ground: 1.8× speed, refill 35/s. Off it: refill 5/s. Wilt ground slows to 0.6× and hurts.
Dodge
2 charges, 1.5 s recharge, 300 ms of invulnerability. A dodge within 150 ms of a hit is perfect: 1.2 s of slow motion on your screen only, double damage.
Latency
Every enemy attack glints for at least 450 ms. The server judges a dodge on your timeline, up to 200 ms back.
Seed shots
One crop gives 3 seeds. Pepper becomes a fire turret, frost melon a slow field, pumpkin a wall, spirit rice a healing totem that revives.
Stats
Rice quality raises permanent stats, with diminishing returns per variety so grinding can't replace skill.
Feel
10 to 20 ms hit-stop, short screen shake, white hit flash, 120 ms input buffer, coyote time on jumps.
Why it looks bad now, and the fix renderer, shading, assets, budgets

The current build is flat-lit boxes seen from far above, with tiny characters and no outlines, sky or grass. The fix:

  • three.js WebGPURenderer with automatic WebGL2 fallback, one shared toon material (ramp, tinted shadows, rim light).
  • Outlines on characters, an edge pass on the world, gradient sky with toon clouds, wind-blown instanced grass, bloom.
  • Characters as VRoid models (@pixiv/three-vrm), animated with Quaternius' free CC0 animation library.
  • Scenery from Quaternius' Stylized Nature MegaKit and KayKit Forest, both CC0.
  • Budgets for a mid laptop: 200 draw calls, 1.5M triangles, 300 MB of textures. You check 60 fps on your laptop; the agents' software renderer can't.

Closest reference: Messenger by Abeto, a cel-shaded multiplayer three.js world that runs on phones.

What stays from the current build server, saves, sign-in, tests

Stays: the PartyServer room, Convex world saves, Clerk sign-in, the 20-tick deterministic simulation and its replay tests, and the dev scripts. Goes: the voxel block world, the Gloomling tower, Sight, Ley logic and the high camera.

Risks five, each with a response
  • Agents can't sculpt anime characters. Placeholders keep work moving until your VRoid hero arrives.
  • Astra's taste may drift. The Look step stops for your approval, and I compare screenshots at every step.
  • Friends far from the server will lag. Slow splashes and long attack glints hide up to about 250 ms.
  • Open world, story, farming and combat is a lot. Each step is playable, so we can stop or reorder anywhere.
  • Frame rate on real laptops is unmeasured. You check it after the Look step.
