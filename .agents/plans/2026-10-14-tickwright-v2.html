All the rules with numbers tick order, spawning, items, Ley, Folk, Strain
Tick order
20 ticks per second, 8 phases in a fixed, printed order: inputs, scheduled blocks, Ley, random ticks, entities, spawning, items, Folk. Randomness is seeded per world, tick and column, so a build gives the same result every time.
Spawning
One attempt per loaded column per tick. Needs a solid opaque floor, 2 air above, block light 0, sky light 4 or less, no player within 24 blocks, one within 128, and the world under 30 hostiles per player.
Items
Real objects with gravity and friction. They merge within 0.5 blocks and vanish after 5 minutes. A Collector moves 1 item every 8 ticks.
Ley logic
Signal 0 to 15, losing 1 per block. Lever, button, pressure plate, light sensor, Reader (comparator), Watcher (observer), repeater, inverter, lamp, piston, sticky piston, Gel block, Launcher, Anchor.
Folk
A bed and a workstation within 48 blocks set the profession. They work, gather and sleep on a schedule. Prices rise when trades sell out and restock twice a day.
Guardians
Checked every second: 3 or more Folk within 16 blocks, slept in the last day, a hostile within 8, no Guardian seen in 30 s.
Strain
More than 24 entities in one block take crush damage. Over 300 in a column calls a Null rift after a 30 s warning. This turns the server's limits into a rule you design around.
Law Shards
Endgame boss drops. Each one rewrites one rule inside your claim, such as spawning at light 3 or doubled item lifetime.

  • Run 6.5 m/s, sprint 9, slide that keeps momentum, double jump, two dash charges with 150 ms invulnerability, a grapple that pulls or swings, and later a glider with rocket boosts.
  • Punch an incoming projectile within 150 ms to send it back at double damage and refill a dash.

  • Kills burst into item fountains with rarity beams. A style meter from D to SSS raises drop rates by up to 50%.

Multiplayer is private co-op for 8

Each world is a room for up to 8 friends, joined by invite link, drop in and out. The world, its farms and the story are shared; each player keeps their own gear and level.

How it works under the hood servers, saves, lag
  • One server per world (a Cloudflare Durable Object) runs the real simulation 20 times a second, near whoever opened it.
  • Only the area within 4 chunks of a player, or held by an Anchor, keeps running. The world pauses when everyone leaves.
  • Changed chunks save to Convex every 30 seconds and when the last player leaves, never every tick.
  • Your own movement and shots show instantly on your screen. The server checks hits by rewinding enemies to where you saw them, up to 200 ms back.
  • Other players show 100 ms behind so they move smoothly. Enemy attacks wind up for at least 400 ms, so a friend on another continent (150 to 250 ms) can still dodge.
  • Online play needs a sign-in through Clerk. Guests can play only on your own computer.

Characters come from VRoid

VRoid Studio is pixiv's free anime character maker: sliders for the face and body, paint-on hair and clothes. It saves a .vrm file that the game loads directly.

Agents can't use a drawing app well, so Astra starts with a free placeholder. Making yours takes about an hour, and every player will be able to import their own .vrm as their avatar.

Story and world the Codex, the Null, six regions

The world runs on the Codex, a law engine ticking 20 times a second. You can see its rules, which makes you a Tickwright, a crime to the Archive that guards it. The Null deletes whatever costs the most to simulate, and it is eating the outer regions.

  • Pell, your mentor, lost her Sight and hides that she once made the Null worse.
  • Ren, an Archive agent, patches a rule in chapter 2 and your farm stops working until you find a new design. She becomes an uneasy ally.
  • Bit, a tiny glitch sprite, tags along and rarely talks.
  • Regions: Hoshino Meadows, Lantern Marsh, Kitsune Forest, Ember Peaks, Yomi, Archive Capital. Each brings one new rule and a boss that drops a Law Shard.
  • The world is 2 km across, with a landmark visible from every peak. Scenes run 30 to 90 s and can always be skipped.
